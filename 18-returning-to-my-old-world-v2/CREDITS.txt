Visuals: everything was made for this video. The world (old-generation terrain with cliffs, overhangs, caves, a
waterfall and floating islands), the house, the wolf, the textures (original 16 px pixel art drawn procedurally to feel
like the classic beta-era look), the menu screens and their pixel font, and the renderer (classic block light and sky
light flood-filled and baked with smooth lighting, flat face shading, a sunset sky and fog, blocky clouds, depth of
field), rendered at 60 fps with smooth spline camera moves, then graded (soft bloom, warmth, grain, vignette).
The player model uses the channel's own character skin.

Typography: Cinzel by the Cinzel Project Authors (Natanael Gama), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see source/fonts).

Music: original piano piece composed and synthesised for this video (no samples).

Sound effects:
- Footsteps and wood sounds: "Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud" by TinyWorlds, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud
- The menu click, the door hinge, the dog's whimpers and panting, the crickets and the low hits are synthesised.

This is a fan-made animation in a blocky-game style; it is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.
