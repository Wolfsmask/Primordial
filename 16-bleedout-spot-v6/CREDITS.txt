Visuals: the valley (mirror lake, river, forests, the snowy range and its waterfalls), the sky (moons, aurora, stars), all
textures, the character animation and the renderer were made for this video.

Shader look: the renderer is a small shader-pack pipeline written for this video (HDR, physically based sky, volumetric
mist and clouds with multiple-scattering octaves and the "powder" term, aerial perspective with sun shafts, crepuscular
rays, mirror reflections on the lake, bloom, Lottes filmic tonemap). Several of these techniques and constants are
adapted from Photon Shaders by SixthSurge (https://github.com/sixthsurge/photon), whose license allows use for published
videos. Photon itself only runs inside Minecraft Java; nothing here runs Minecraft.

Music: "Feel Good Inc." (Gorillaz), 0:59.85 to 1:15.95. The song file was supplied by the channel. It is a commercial
recording and is not licensed by this project: on YouTube, adding it from the Shorts audio library (or accepting a
Content ID claim) is the usual way to use it.
