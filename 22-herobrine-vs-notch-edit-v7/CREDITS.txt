Characters: the Herobrine and Notch skins were supplied by the channel (64 x 64 skins, source/web/herobrine2.png and
source/web/notch2.png).

Visuals: everything else was made for this video: the night castle courtyard (broken curtain wall, gatehouse towers,
a lit keep, an obsidian portal, banners, braziers), the burning village and forest around it, the mountains on the
horizon, the block textures, the hand-keyed animation on a bendy blocky rig, and the renderer: three.js with physically
based lighting, soft shadows, bloom, glows on the models themselves, an anime line teleport, a barrier that cracks and
shatters, lightning, sparks, smoke and fire. Rendered at 60 fps and upscaled to 1080 x 1920, with an anime-style edit
pass (speed ramps, impact frames, speed lines, zoom punches, shake and chromatic aberration).

Typography: Cinzel by the Cinzel Project Authors (Natanael Gama), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see source/web).
