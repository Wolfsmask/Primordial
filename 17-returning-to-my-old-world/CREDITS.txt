Visuals: everything was made for this video. The world, the house, the wolf, the textures (original 16 px pixel art,
drawn procedurally in an older blocky-game style), the menu screens and their pixel font, and the renderer (classic
block light and sky light flood-filled and baked with smooth lighting, flat face shading, linear fog, square sun,
blocky clouds), rendered at 60 fps with eased spline camera motion.
The player arm uses the channel's own character skin.

Music: original piano piece composed and synthesised for this video (no samples).

Sound effects:
- Footsteps: "Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud" by TinyWorlds, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud
- The menu click, the door hinge and the dog's whimpers and panting are synthesised.

Captions: Inter (SIL Open Font License).
