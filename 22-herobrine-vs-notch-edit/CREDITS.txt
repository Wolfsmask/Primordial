Visuals: everything was made for this video. The two fighters (original character designs drawn as 64 x 64 skins: an
ash-skinned hooded figure with white eyes, and a white-and-gold creator with golden eyes), their capes and blades, the
night castle courtyard (a broken curtain wall with battlements, gatehouse towers, round corner towers and a lit keep, an

3D chrome typography. Rendered at 60 fps, 1080 x 1920, with beat-synced edit effects (zoom punches, flashes, shake,
