Visuals: everything was made for this video. The End is rebuilt from scratch and generated in Python as voxels:
the main island with the Rift Gate (an obsidian ring with gold inlay, runes and chevrons over a stepped plinth), a
lamp-lit causeway, guardian obelisks and rune pillars with crystals; an End City of pagoda towers with lit windows,
eaves, banners, spires and bridges; crystal spires of violet and teal hexagonal crystals; an elder grove with a giant
glowing tree, hanging vines and a pool that spills into the void as a light fall; and the far End out to 1.5 km
(islands, broken towers, colossal pillars). The block textures are original 16 x 16 pixel art, with normal,
roughness and metalness maps painted for them.

The renderer is a custom shader pipeline (three.js): physically based blocks with sky reflections and soft shadows, a
black hole in the sky lensed with a precomputed Schwarzschild ray trace (its accretion disk, Doppler beaming and the
far side bent over the top), a nebula and galactic band, volumetric mist with shadowed light shafts, atmospheric
perspective, camera motion blur, depth of field, bloom with lens dirt, anamorphic streaks and a filmic grade.

The phone lock screen, notifications, caption and the blocky title lettering are original designs drawn for this
video. Interface and caption type: Inter by Rasmus Andersson, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Music: "Undress Rehearsal (Hardtekk Super Slowed)" by VXLTAGE, the track supplied by the channel, edited for this
video (a tape stop before the drop, three bars cut from the drop, the break pulled under water, an echo-out ending).
