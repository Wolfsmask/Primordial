Visuals: everything was made for this video. The End is rebuilt from scratch: a main island with obsidian pillars,
crystals and the exit portal, and floating isles round it (a grove of glowing trees, an end city of purpur towers,
crystal spires, a chorus forest, drifting debris, a gateway), all generated in Python and meshed as voxels with
ambient occlusion. The block textures are original 16 x 16 pixel art. The dragon is a blocky model built from boxes.
The renderer is a shader pipeline in the spirit of the one made for the bleedout spot video, designed for the End: an
HDR scene, a void sky with a nebula, stars and an eclipsed sun with its corona, a glowing void sea far below, void mist
raymarched with in-scattering from the eclipse and the crystals, crepuscular rays, a bloom pyramid and a filmic
tonemap. The phone lock screen and notifications are an original design drawn for this video.
